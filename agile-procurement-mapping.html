How to read it: the blue spine and the green agile cycle are identical in every path; the work cannot push past a column until that phase's amber gate is cleared. The path you follow is set by project value and complexity (PCRA), which in turn sets the approval authority and the gate package.

Governance model: the SRB is the project-level body on every path. It provides corporate challenge and endorses documentation (BCA, PSOR, Corporate Submission, IOC / FOC Certificates, PCR) before escalation. The higher boards approve at the gateways: DCB approves the BCA in Options Analysis on all paths; departmental approval is PMB (Path B) or PMB + IRMC (Paths C and D); final project / expenditure authority is L1 (A), DM (B), MND (C), or TB Ministers (D).

Note on placement: deliverables have been placed in the phase where P2MF normally sequences them (business case in Options Analysis; baseline documents in Definition; corporate submission, approval and TBS reporting in Implementation). Adjust if your authoritative mapping differs.

P2MF agility: the agile steps can use tailored Delegations of Authority (DoA) or the Tailored Project Approval Process (TPAP) to scale AI from prototype to production without waterfall delays. The conflict methodology favours speed of execution over perfection, with documentation scaled to project risk.

Responsible AI: Agile Step 8 (QA / Ethics layer) enforces the principle to build responsible AI in from the start. Bias, explainability and ethical risk are reviewed (by the SRB on Path A) before models are pushed to live environments.
